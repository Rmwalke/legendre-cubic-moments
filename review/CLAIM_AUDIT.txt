1. IRREDUCIBILITY AUDIT AND INTEGRATION

The proof in the preceding p2 research note is logically sound under its
explicit Scholl/Hodge and local-at-3 inputs. Its rank-one character lemma
is correctly restricted to G_Q and Q_ell-valued characters. The theorem
of Tate in Serre, Chapter III, section 1.2, printed page III-7, identifies
an abelian Hodge--Tate local representation with a locally algebraic one
when inertia is semisimple. This was checked directly in the primary scan:
https://www.math.mcgill.ca/darmon/courses/18-19/gs/serre-mcgill.pdf
(PDF page index 98). A character has the required semisimplicity.
Kronecker--Weber then proves that division by its Tate power leaves a
finite-order global character. No claim of this form for arbitrary number
fields is used.

The single length-four Jordan block has a unique invariant subspace of
each dimension. Consequently an absolutely invariant subspace descends
to Q_ell as ker(N^r). Its determinant must have Tate exponent divisible
by 5, whereas the local Frobenius flag forces exponents 1, 3, or 6.
These are incompatible. For restriction to any finite extension, passage
to its Galois closure makes conjugate subspaces stable under the same
normal subgroup; the unique Jordan flag then makes the subspace globally
stable. This validates the strong irreducibility corollary without
extending the rank-one lemma to that number field.

4. NORMALIZATION MAP: THE TWO DIFFERENT USES OF 'PLUS'

Literal manuscript W4+ means I=+1 and has inert trace -S_p-1,
P3(T)=1-3T and conductor432.
Literal manuscript W4- means I=-1 and has inert trace +S_p+1,
P3(T)=1 and conductor1296.
The older numerical 'plus-sign recipe' refers to the positive inert trace
+S_p+1. It therefore matches the literal W4- descent, NOT W4+.
At split primes the two have the same traces, so the distinction cannot
be resolved from split data alone. The conductor and local-at-3 statements
must be attached to the literal inversion eigenspaces throughout.
